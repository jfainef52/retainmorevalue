Estate Assumptions

Non-spouse beneficiary, SECURE Act 10-year rule. Enter the beneficiary's age today — their age at inheritance is derived automatically from the account holder's current age and the Horizon Age below, so this field doesn't need updating every time the horizon changes. The account owner is always well past the required beginning date at the modeled horizon, so the final IRS regulations (effective 2025) require the beneficiary to take annual life-expectancy RMDs in years 1–9 (IRS Table I, reduce-by-one method) and empty the account by year 10 — not a single lump distribution. The Beneficiary Rate above is applied to each year's forced distribution, stacked on whatever else the beneficiary earns that year; a beneficiary in peak earning years should generally use a top-bracket rate here, not their everyday rate.
